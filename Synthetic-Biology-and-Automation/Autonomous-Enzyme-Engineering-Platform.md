# A Generalized Platform for Artificial Intelligence-Powered Autonomous Enzyme Engineering

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-61209-y

A closed-loop platform combining protein language models, supervised machine learning, and biofoundry automation to engineer enzymes with minimal human intervention.
It requires only a protein sequence and a measurable fitness assay: unsupervised prediction generates the initial variant library, the biofoundry builds and assays it, and the resulting data trains a supervised model that proposes the next round. 
Applied to *Arabidopsis thaliana* halide methyltransferase and *Yersinia mollaretii* phytase, it produced 90-fold and 26-fold improvements, respectively, within four engineering rounds while testing fewer than 500 variants per enzyme.

**Keywords:** Autonomous Experimentation, Enzyme Engineering, Protein Language Models, Biofoundry, Laboratory Automation, Machine Learning
