## Extensive individual and microorganism-specific circadian oscillations of the upper respiratory tract microbiome

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2026.117284

A study showing that the upper respiratory microbiome exhibits strong circadian oscillations, with sampling time influencing microbial profiles and potentially introducing false positives in microbiome analyses.

**Keywords:** Respiratory Microbiome, Circadian Rhythm, Oropharynx, Metagenomics, Biomarkers, Sampling Bias
