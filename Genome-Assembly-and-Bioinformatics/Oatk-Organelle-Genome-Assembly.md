## Oatk: a de novo assembly tool for complex plant organelle genomes

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-025-03676-6

A study combining specialized assembly strategies and HMM-based organelle identification to improve reconstruction of complex plant organelle genomes.

**Keywords:** Genome Assembly, Organelle Genomes, HMM, HiFi Reads, Bioinformatics, Plant Genomics
