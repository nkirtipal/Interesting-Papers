## Incomplete recovery and individualized responses of the human distal gut microbiota to repeated antibiotic perturbation

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1000087107

A study showing that antibiotic treatment can perturb the gut microbiome, leading to incomplete recovery and individualized responses across individuals.

**Keywords:** Microbiome, Antibiotics, Gut Microbiota, Perturbation, Resilience, Alternative Stable States
