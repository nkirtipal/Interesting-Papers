# Rapid Directed Evolution Guided by Protein Language Models and Epistatic Interactions

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aea1820

MULTI-evolve, a framework combining protein language models with neural network epistatic modeling to design synergistic multimutants in a single round of directed evolution, rather than the usual stepwise stacking across multiple rounds. Achieved >100-fold catalytic improvement in APEX and 10-fold in CRISPR-Cas13d.

**Keywords:** Protein Engineering, Protein Language Models, Directed Evolution, Epistasis, Deep Learning
