# Experimental Assessment of AI-Based Interactome Mapping

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-70942-x

An experimental benchmark of AlphaFold-based interactome prediction against reference interactome maps in yeast and human. 
High-confidence predictions match experimental quality, but proteome-wide virtual screening identifies far fewer novel protein-protein interactions than experimental mapping the yeast interactome map reported here identifies over 40-fold more. 
The authors conclude that AI's current strength lies in structural modeling of experimentally identified interactions rather than interaction discovery.

**Keywords:** Protein-Protein Interactions, Interactome, AlphaFold, Structure Prediction, Benchmarking, Yeast
