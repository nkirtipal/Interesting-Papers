## Nicheformer

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-025-02814-z

A transformer-based foundation model trained on more than 110 million dissociated and spatially resolved cells across 73 tissues. 
Nicheformer learns spatially aware cell representations and enables the prediction of spatial cellular context from scRNA-seq data, 
demonstrating the value of integrating spatial transcriptomics into biological foundation models.

**Keywords:** Foundation Models, Spatial Transcriptomics, Single-Cell RNA-seq, Transformers, Representation Learning, Computational Biology
