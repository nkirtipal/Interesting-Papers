# UniPert-G2CP Bridges Genetic and Chemical Screens from Molecular Representation to Phenotype Modeling

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2026.06.005

A two-stage deep learning framework that unifies genetic and chemical perturbations within a shared molecular representation space and enabling genetic-to-chemical transfer learning to predict phenotypic effects. 
By pretraining on large-scale genetic perturbation screens and fine-tuning on chemical perturbations, UniPert-G2CP improves in silico drug screening while capturing context-dependent cellular responses across diverse biological contexts.

**Keywords:** Deep Learning, Perturbation Modeling, Transfer Learning, Drug Discovery, AI Virtual Cell, Precision Medicine
