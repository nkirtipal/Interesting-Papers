## Disordered Microbial Communities in Asthmatic Airways

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0008578

A study showing that the bronchial tree is not sterile and that airway microbial communities differ between healthy individuals and patients with asthma or COPD.

**Keywords:** Lung Microbiome, Asthma, COPD, Airway Microbiota, Respiratory Microbiology
