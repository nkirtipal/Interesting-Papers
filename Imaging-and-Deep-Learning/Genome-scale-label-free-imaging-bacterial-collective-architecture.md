### Genome-scale label-free imaging reveals cellular physiology encoded in bacterial collective architecture

**Mellick et al. (2026)** — bioRxiv preprint

**DOI:** [https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.08.30.748126](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.08.30.748126)

Genome-scale label-free imaging is used to characterize bacterial collective architecture and connect large-scale morphological patterns with cellular physiology. The study combines quantitative imaging and computational analysis to extract biologically meaningful phenotypes from bacterial communities.

**Keywords:** Label-Free Imaging, Bacterial Physiology, Quantitative Imaging, Computational Biology, Deep Learning
