# A Biofoundry Workflow for the Identification of Genetic Determinants of Microbial Growth Inhibition

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1093/synbio/ysab004

An automated biofoundry workflow applied to molecular microbiology, screening 2,880 *Pseudomonas* transposon mutants against the potato pathogen *Streptomyces scabies* and completing the workflow in approximately two weeks. Running the assays in individual 24-well plates avoided the neighbouring-colony interference that makes pin-replicator screens unreliable for diffusible metabolites, and identified a biosynthetic gene cluster that standard genome-mining tools had missed because it lacks the class-defining enzymes those tools search for.

**Keywords:** Biofoundry, Laboratory Automation, High-Throughput Screening, Biosynthetic Gene Clusters, Synthetic Biology, Microbial Interactions
